What Exelidoc does

An on-demand writing assistant for Gmail and Google Docs.

Browser Extension

Gmail & Google Docs

Open the Exelidoc panel beside a draft or document, enter an instruction, and ask it to revise text or create new content. Changes are applied when you click Ask, with an Undo option.

AI on request

Your request is sent to Exelidoc's hosted backend and AI provider. The service may take time to respond, especially while it is running on free-tier infrastructure.

Data handling

Know what you send

Only submit text you want the AI to process. Requests go to Exelidoc's backend and AI provider; account details and request usage are also processed to provide the service and enforce plan limits.

Subscription

Plans with monthly request limits

Free includes 50 AI requests per 30-day period. Paid plan limits, prices, and subscription terms are shown at checkout.

Ready to try it?

Install the Chrome/Edge extension and create your free account in its popup.

What Exelidoc does

AI grammar and data corrections, applied where you already write.

Browser Extension

Gmail & Google Docs

Watches your compose box and document as you write, and flags grammar and clarity issues automatically -- no separate tab, no copy-pasting into another tool.

Office Add-in

Word, Excel, PowerPoint & Outlook

One add-in, all four apps. In Excel it corrects data-entry cells automatically as you edit. In Word and PowerPoint it checks as your selection moves. In Outlook it checks while the pane is open.

Cloud AI

Fast, hosted correction

Runs on a cloud AI model rather than your local machine -- no large downloads, no GPU required, works the same on any computer.

Data handling

Your content isn't stored

Exelidoc logs that a request happened (for usage limits), not the content of what you wrote. Your text is sent to the AI provider to be corrected and is not retained by Exelidoc afterward.

Excel specifically

Formulas and numbers are never touched

Only text-looking cells are ever sent for correction. Formulas and numeric values are always left exactly as you entered them.

Subscription

One plan, cancel anytime

A single flat monthly subscription covers every app -- browser extension and Office add-in both, no separate purchases.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes the Exelidoc browser extension, website, and separately distributed Office add-in preview.

Information we process

  • Email address, account identifier, session token, subscription status, and plan tier.
  • Request counts and AI usage records used to operate accounts and enforce plan limits.
  • Text from the current Google Docs document, Gmail draft, or other selected content and instructions you choose to submit.
  • Your question, approved source links, and validated Google source IDs for follow-ups. Exelidoc history does not retain source text or AI responses.
  • Payment and billing details handled by Stripe. Exelidoc does not receive your full card number.

How text is used

When you generate a preview or request an edit, the text and instruction you submit are sent to the Exelidoc backend and configured AI provider to produce a response. Google Docs currently sends the full plain-text document, not only a selected passage. Exelidoc history displays and stores your question and may store validated source links and Google source IDs so an explicit follow-up can re-fetch the source. It does not store source text or AI responses in history. The AI provider may process or retain requests under its own terms and settings. Exelidoc does not promise that submitted text is immediately erased from provider or service logs.

Browser storage

The extension stores your account session token and email locally in Chrome or Edge extension storage so it can associate requests with your account. When Google access is connected, it also indexes inbox messages locally in the extension's IndexedDB, up to 20,000 characters per message, and refreshes the index every 5 minutes while Chrome is running. This indexing does not send message content to Gemini. Email content is sent to Gemini only when the user asks Exelidoc to use selected matching emails for a draft or spreadsheet task. Google authorization allows Gmail reading and spreadsheet editing; spreadsheet changes still require user review and approval. Disconnecting Google clears the local index; the Google authorization can also be revoked in your Google account settings. Anyone with access to your signed-in browser profile may be able to access locally indexed messages or use the session.

Payments

Paid subscriptions use Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes payment details and sends subscription events to Exelidoc so the account plan can be updated. You can manage an eligible subscription through the extension's Manage subscription control. See Stripe's privacy policy for its data practices.

Sharing and service providers

Information is shared with the backend host, database provider, AI provider, and Stripe as needed to provide Exelidoc, maintain accounts, process requests, and bill subscriptions. These providers have their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

You can clear saved questions and source references from the extension or request account deletion there. Deleting your account removes the Exelidoc account and account-linked history and usage records. Active paid subscriptions must first be canceled through Stripe; account deletion becomes available after Exelidoc receives confirmation that the subscription has ended. Stripe may retain billing records for its own legal and operational requirements. Service providers may retain data in logs or backups for limited periods; submitted text is not guaranteed to be immediately erased from their systems. A specific published retention schedule still needs to be established before broad commercial launch.

Security

The extension stores your session token in local browser extension storage. Protect access to your browser profile and sign out on shared devices. Password reset links are single-use and expire after 30 minutes. Do not submit sensitive or confidential information unless you are authorized and comfortable with the providers' data practices.

Contact

For beta support or privacy requests, use the Exelidoc project support page. A dedicated customer support contact should replace this channel before commercial launch.

This policy describes current behavior and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal review. Confirm the providers, retention periods, and applicable privacy obligations before commercial launch.

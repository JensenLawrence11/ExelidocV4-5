Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes the Exelidoc browser extension, website, and experimental Office add-in preview.

Information Exelidoc processes

  • Account email, account identifier, password hash, session token, subscription status, and plan tier.
  • Request counts and AI usage records used to operate accounts and enforce plan limits.
  • Text and instructions you choose to submit from Google Docs, Gmail drafts, selected Gmail messages, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
  • Your question and validated source links and Google source IDs for follow-ups. History does not retain source text or AI responses.
  • Payment and billing information handled by Stripe. Exelidoc does not receive your full card number.
  • Hashed, short-lived rate-limit keys for authentication abuse prevention. Raw email addresses, IP addresses, and reset tokens are not stored in the rate-limit table.

Google data and AI requests

After you connect Google, Exelidoc can read Gmail and Google Drive data, read and edit Google Docs and Sheets, and read Google Slides. Gmail inbox indexing runs locally in the extension every five minutes while the browser is running. The local index stores up to 20,000 body characters per message in the browser profile; it is not automatically sent to Exelidoc or Gemini.

Searching connected Google sources happens when you ask. When you select source content and explicitly request an AI response, that content and your instruction are sent to the Exelidoc backend and Google Gemini to fulfill the request. Google Docs requests currently include the full plain-text document. Spreadsheet requests include the selected cell range. Exelidoc history stores your question and safe source references, not source text or Gemini's response. Gemini processes submitted content under Google's applicable terms and settings; Exelidoc cannot promise immediate deletion from a provider's or hosting service's logs or backups.

Google data is used to provide the features you request, not for advertising or sale. Document and spreadsheet edits are previewed for your approval. Gmail drafts are not sent by Exelidoc; the extension only applies approved text to the open draft.

Browser storage

The extension stores your account session token and email in local browser extension storage. The Office preview stores its session token in that Office host's local storage. Disconnecting Google in Exelidoc clears the local Gmail index. Revoking Google access from your Google account stops future API access, but use Exelidoc's disconnect control to clear the cached index as well. Removing browser data or uninstalling the extension also removes its local storage. Anyone with access to your signed-in browser or Office profile may be able to access locally stored data or use the session.

Account email and operational monitoring

Exelidoc sends verification, welcome, and password-reset emails through Resend or the SMTP provider configured by the operator. Verification links expire after 24 hours; password-reset links expire after 30 minutes and are single-use. The token is stored as a hash and carried in a URL fragment so the initial page request does not send it to the API server.

The backend uses Supabase for account, history, usage, rate-limit, and monthly AI-budget records, and Render for hosting. If Sentry monitoring is configured, it receives application error types and stack traces plus static AI-budget alerts. Exelidoc removes request details, user identity, breadcrumbs, extra fields, and exception messages before sending events; request content, source text, email addresses, and tokens are not intentionally sent to Sentry. Sentry's own retention and alert settings are controlled by the operator.

Payments

Paid subscriptions use Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes payment details and sends subscription events to Exelidoc so the account plan can be updated. You can manage an eligible subscription through the extension's Manage subscription control. See Stripe's privacy policy for its data practices.

Sharing and service providers

Exelidoc shares information with Google APIs and Gemini, Render, Supabase, Stripe, Resend or the configured SMTP provider, and Sentry when monitoring is enabled, as needed to provide features, host the service, manage accounts, deliver email, process payments, and diagnose errors. These providers have their own terms, security controls, and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

  • The local Gmail index remains in your browser profile until you disconnect Google in Exelidoc or remove the extension/browser data. It does not have a separate automatic expiry.
  • Saved questions and source references remain in account history until you clear history or delete your account. History does not automatically expire.
  • Deleting an Exelidoc account removes its account-linked history and AI request-usage records. An active paid subscription must first be canceled and confirmed as ended. Stripe may retain billing information for legal and operational purposes.
  • Verification and password-reset links become unusable at their stated expiry and are deleted when used or checked as expired. Monthly AI-budget totals are aggregate records without a user identifier.
  • Google, Gemini, hosting, email, payment, monitoring, and database providers may keep data in their own logs or backups under their terms and retention settings. Deletion from Exelidoc does not guarantee immediate deletion from those systems.

Security

Exelidoc uses single-use hashed password-reset tokens, shared server-side rate limits, a $10 USD monthly Gemini spend ceiling, and reviewed changes for documents, sheets, and drafts. Protect your browser and Office profiles, sign out on shared devices, and do not submit information unless you are authorized and comfortable with the providers' data practices.

Contact

For beta support or privacy requests, use the Exelidoc project support page. A dedicated customer support contact and jurisdiction-specific legal review are still needed before broad commercial launch.

This policy describes current product behavior and is not a substitute for legal advice. Review it against the actual provider settings, service contracts, and applicable privacy obligations before commercial launch.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What we collect

Exelidoc processes your email address, account/session information, subscription status, request counts, and AI usage records to provide the service and enforce plan limits. Payment processing is handled by Stripe. Text you submit from Gmail or Google Docs is sent to the Exelidoc backend and AI provider to fulfill your request.

What we send to the AI provider

Text submitted to Exelidoc is sent to its configured AI provider and a response is returned to the extension. Review the AI provider's terms and data practices before submitting sensitive information. Do not submit confidential data unless you are authorized to do so.

Payment information

Billing is handled entirely by Stripe. Exelidoc never sees or stores your card details.

Your account

Exelidoc creates an account token automatically when you sign up from the extension. It is stored locally in the browser extension and used to associate requests with your account. Keep your browser profile private; anyone with access to the signed-in browser may use your account.

Deleting your data

Self-service account deletion is not currently available. Contact the project owner through the repository linked on the Download page to ask about account deletion.

Contact

For project questions, use the repository linked on the Download page.

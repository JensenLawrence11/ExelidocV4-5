Gmail / Docs

Write with an assistant

Open Exelidoc beside a Gmail draft or Google Doc, enter an instruction, and ask it to revise text or create new content. Changes are applied when you choose Ask, with an Undo option.

Your writing stays yours

Nothing changes until you ask

Exelidoc does not edit as you type. Review the request in its panel, click Ask to apply it, and use Undo to restore the previous draft.

Privacy

Requests happen on demand

Exelidoc sends the text or selected cells you ask it to work on to its service and AI provider. It does not continuously edit your writing as you type.

Get started with the extension

Install Exelidoc, open its toolbar popup, and create your free account there. Your session is saved automatically in the extension.
